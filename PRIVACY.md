# OpenTierBoy Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: [the 2.0.0 release date, set at the cutover]**

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information
when you use the Service and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

## Hosting Provider

Our Service is hosted by Vercel. For details on how Vercel collects and uses data, please refer
to [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Collection of Information

### Information You Provide to Us

We do not collect any personal data from users directly through the Service.

### Information Collected Automatically

We do not collect any information directly. Any automatic collection of anonymized data, such as page views and usage
metrics, is performed by Vercel in accordance with their privacy practices. This data does not include personal
identifiers and is used for improving the Service.

### Data Kept in Your Browser

The Service keeps some data in your browser, on your device, so that it works as you expect. This data stays on your
device and is not shared with us or any third parties.

- **Local storage** holds your appearance choices (Light, Dark or System mode, game colours, and the Modern or Classic
  font), the lists you opened recently and the ones you pinned ("Recently viewed"), the websites whose images you chose
  to always load, and the ids of pictures your browser had to remove for lack of space. It can also hold pictures
  that older versions of the Service saved there, until the Service can move them to IndexedDB.
- **IndexedDB** holds the pictures you upload, paste or drop from your device, so that your lists still show them on
  your next visit.

You can remove all of this data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

## Use of Information

- Providing, maintaining, and improving our services;
- Monitoring and analyzing trends, usage, and activities in connection with our services.

By accessing and using our services, you consent to the processing and transfer of your information by Vercel in
accordance with their privacy practices.

## Sharing of Information

We do not share any personal information directly. However, Vercel may share anonymized data as part of their standard
operations. For detailed information on how Vercel handles data, please refer
to [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Images in Your Tier Lists

OpenTierBoy lets you add pictures from your device and images from other websites to your tier lists. We handle them as
follows:

1. **Pictures from your device**: Pictures you upload, paste or drop stay in your browser (see "Data Kept in Your
   Browser"). They are never sent to our servers. A shared link holds only an id for each picture, so people who open
   your link see a tile with the picture's label in its place.
2. **Freeze Frame files**: A Freeze Frame picture you download also holds your list and your device pictures, so that it
   can reopen as an editable list. Anyone you give the file to receives those pictures.
3. **Images from other websites**: When you add an image by its address, the address is written into your list's link.
   Your browser loads the image straight from that website, without telling it which page you came from. Images from
   websites on our trusted list load at once; images from any other website load only after you allow them. The website
   that hosts an image can see your browser's request, as with any image on the web, and its own privacy policy applies.
4. **Link previews**: When a link to a list is shared in a chat or on a social network, our server draws a preview
   picture of the list. To draw it, our server fetches the images that come from websites on our trusted list. It never
   fetches images from any other website, and it never receives your device pictures, which appear as label tiles.
5. **Responsibility**: You are responsible for the content of the images you add. We do not monitor or moderate them.
6. **Removal**: We do not store your pictures on our servers, so we cannot remove them. You can remove them from your
   device by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Images from other websites stay on those websites.
