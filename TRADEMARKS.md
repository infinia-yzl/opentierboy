The code of OpenTierBoy is open source under the [AGPL-3.0 license](LICENSE). You may use, change and share it under
that license. The license covers the code. It gives no right to use the OpenTierBoy name, logo or wordmark, which
belong to Isaac Yong.

## What you may do

- Say, truthfully, that your project is based on OpenTierBoy or uses its code, for example "based on OpenTierBoy".
- Link to [opentierboy.com](https://opentierboy.com) and to this repository.
- Use the name and logo to talk about OpenTierBoy itself, for example in an article or a video about it.

## What you may not do

- Run a fork or a copy under the name OpenTierBoy, or under a name or logo that people could mistake for it.
- Use the OpenTierBoy name, logo or wordmark in a way that suggests we made, approved or support your project, its
  content or its tier lists.
- Show the OpenTierBoy logo or wordmark next to content that breaks our [Terms of Service](TERMS.md), such as illegal,
  hateful or sexual content.

## If you fork OpenTierBoy

Give your fork its own name and logo before you put it online. The name and logo appear in these places:

- `public/brand/` (the logo and wordmark files) and the icons in `app/` and `public/`;
- the site name and descriptions in `app/layout.tsx` and `app/manifest.ts`;
- the wordmark in the header, in Zen, in link previews (`app/api/og/`) and in Freeze Frame pictures
  (`components/ListCard.tsx`);
- the About page, the blog, `README.md`, `TERMS.md` and `PRIVACY.md`, which name OpenTierBoy and its owner.
